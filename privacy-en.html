Privacy Policy

DroneKoll — last updated: 2026-08-08

Summary

Everything you enter stays on your device. We do not sell, share or store your data on our servers. The only data that leaves your device is crash reports — see below.

Location data

The app uses your GPS position to display relevant weather and air traffic information nearby. Your location never leaves your device — it is not sent to any servers and is not stored permanently.

The app makes anonymous API requests to open services (Open-Meteo, adsb.fi, Nominatim/OpenStreetMap) using your approximate position. These services may log IP addresses according to their own terms.

Flight log

Logged flights (time, location, drone) are stored only locally on your device via the device's standard storage. No data is synced to the cloud or shared with third parties.

Drone certificate

Images you upload are stored only locally on your device.

Pro profile (pilot details)

If you use Pro mode, the details you enter — name, email address, phone number, certificate numbers, operator ID, insurance details and your signature — are stored in your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). They are used to fill in your flight log, drone card and PDF exports. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Settings and preferences

App settings (e.g. selected country, drone model) are stored locally in the app's settings on your device and are not transmitted.

Third-party services

The app communicates with the following external services. These services process requests (potentially including IP addresses) according to their own privacy policies:

  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts
  • adsb.fi — real-time air traffic (ADS-B)
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim — map tiles and geocoding
  • RainViewer — precipitation radar
  • FAA Aviation Weather Center — NOTAM information

No personal data is transmitted to these services. Requests contain only geographic coordinates to retrieve location-based data.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) for crash analysis. When the app crashes, the following technical data is sent to Google's servers:

  • Device model and OS version
  • App version
  • Timestamp and stack trace for the crash
  • An installation ID that cannot be linked to you as a person

No location data or personal details are sent. More information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Apart from Crashlytics, the app uses no analytics, tracking or advertising services. No user profiles are created.
